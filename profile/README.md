## About

A reimagining of nitro's earlier track *qualia*, this VARIANT TRANCE piece blends tech dance, hi-tech, and hard trance into an energetic, melodic experience.

Its title combines two fascinating concepts: **qualia**, the subjective qualities of conscious experience, and **ideaesthesia**, the phenomenon in which concepts evoke sensory experiences.

Perhaps music is more than just sound. Perhaps what we hear is never quite the same as what someone else experiences.
